          Project

          Fusion of Computer Science,
          Engineering and Mathematics Approaches
          for Expanding Combinatorial Reconfiguration

          The final goal of this research project is to provide a common infrastructure for utilizing and applying the algorithmic technology of combinatorial reconfiguration. Toward this goal, computer scientists, engineers, and mathematicians work cooperatively, aim at establishing the algorithmic foundation, the implementation technology foundation, and the mathematical foundation of combinatorial reconfiguration.
